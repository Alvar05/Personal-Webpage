Awayter, 2025–26
2025

Dispensing food looks easy.
Until you try it.

For my final-year project as a Junior Engineer at Awayter, I developed an automatic bulk food dispenser. Its rotary valve has four flexible blades and can be removed and taken apart for cleaning. I designed it, 3D-printed it and tested it with different foods.

Reflections were causing false readings in the optical level sensor. I tilted the window by 7.5 degrees and added a light trap opposite it. That solved the problem.
